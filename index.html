Our story

From a Bangarpet chat streets to Heart of New Zealand.

The Bangarpet PaniPuri began as a single market stall in 2005, built on recipes carried over from Bangarpet. Twenty One years on, we're still a small, family-run kitchen — everything made fresh, nothing sitting under a heat lamp.

We're proud to be a New Zealand based business, serving our community one order at a time.
